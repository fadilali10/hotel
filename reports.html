ℹ️ Report Information

This report uses your hotel's invoice, invoice item, and payment information stored in Supabase.

Monthly Earnings = payments received during the selected month.

Total Earnings = all payments received by this hotel.

Room Revenue = invoice items identified as room or stay charges.

Restaurant Revenue = invoice items identified as restaurant, food, or order charges.

Profit Before Expenses currently represents payments received before expenses because an expense management table has not been added.
